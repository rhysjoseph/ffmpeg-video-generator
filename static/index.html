Colour & sound

Source colour primaries and transfer are retained where tagged; untagged material is treated as BT.709. Output is limited-range YUV. HDR is not tone-mapped. An HDR logo requires a separate colour-managed workflow.

MOV uses 48 kHz, 24-bit PCM audio. H.265 proxies use 48 kHz AAC. Each source audio track is retained unless audio is removed. Subtitles, data tracks and chapters are not copied.
